Preprocessing: Extraction and organization of protein-related information from UniProt and PubMed sources.

Text Representation Generation: Transformation of textual information into numerical representations using methods such as TF-IDF, BioSentVec, and BioWordVec.

Result Visualization: Analysis and visualization of protein function prediction results, including statistical significance evaluation.
